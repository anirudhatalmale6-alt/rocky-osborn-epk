Biography

With over 35 years of dedicated stage experience, Rocky Osborn stands out as a highly respected powerhouse in the stand-up comedy circuit. Known for his flawless timing, sharp crowd work, and cross-generational appeal, Rocky effortlessly connects with diverse groups everywhere he performs.

Beyond delivering elite punchlines, Rocky is an industry mover-and-shaker. As the founder and executive producer of Rockys Comedy Live LLC, he actively produces, promotes, and anchors top-tier weekend showcases at premier venues, keeping live entertainment thriving nationwide.
